## Project Title
FloodCast Venice – Water you thinking?

## Description

**What problem is being solved?**  
FloodCast Venice addresses the problem of **increasing relative sea level and flood risk** in Venice. The city is affected both by **global sea-level rise** and **local land subsidence**, but these effects are often hard to visualise and compare across different future scenarios. The tool helps to quantify and communicate how quickly critical flood levels may be reached under defined assumptions.

**What is the practical use case?**  
The web application serves as an **interactive decision-support dashboard** for:

- urban planners and engineers (infrastructure planning, sea wall / MOSE adaptation),
- civil protection and emergency services (flood risk timelines),
- policy makers (prioritising adaptation investments),
- and interested citizens or tourists (understanding long-term flood trends).

Users can explore historical tide gauge data, climate‑driven regression models, and RCP-based sea-level scenarios, and see **when specific flood thresholds are exceeded**.

**What is the expected outcome?**  
The expected outcome is a **Python-based, web-deployed dashboard** that:

- processes **real open-data sources** (tide gauge, global temperature, CO₂, sea-level scenarios),
- applies **linear regression and climate-driven modelling** to estimate future relative sea level in Venice,
- visualises **uncertainty bands** and compares statistics-based trends to physical climate scenarios,
- and outputs **threshold exceedance years** to support evidence-based flood risk management and infrastructure planning.
